Shipped · Capstone

OutLoud — AI English-Speaking Practice

Five minutes of speaking practice on your phone, with nobody listening but you
Problem

College students who read and write English fluently freeze when they have to speak it in presentations, vivas and interviews. Every place to practise has a person in it who might judge them, so they script, skip hard words, get caught, and withdraw.

Research

6 user interviews (5 in segment) and a day-0 baseline: comfort speaking to a stranger averaged 1.6 / 5. Key insight: the fear is of the witness, not the language. Nobody asked for better teaching; they asked for privacy.

What I built

A 5-step loop: prompt card → 15s to think → 60s unscripted speech → AI coaching (one strength, one suggestion, one rewrite) → unlimited retakes that leave no trace. No score, no login, no audience.

Hardest trade-off

Showing no score at all. It protects nervous users, but removes the most legible form of progress. v2 plans an opt-in score unlocked after session three.

Early signals
Market leader: 70% monthly paid churn 71% opened a cold link 1 unprompted return session North Star: Week-4 practice completion

Growth strategy

Airbnb India — Growth Loops for a Low-Take-Rate Market

PLG initiatives, growth loops, four MVP prototypes and a revenue model built for India
Diagnosis

Four constraints bind Airbnb India: trust (listing ≠ reality), frequency (1–2 trips a year), thin leaky supply, and a 15.5% take rate on a ₹4,000 room night. I audited what Airbnb already ships before proposing anything.

Five moves
  • Product-led ops (AI listing copilot, WhatsApp self-serve support)
  • Proof of Stay: verified guest photos in reviews
  • Trust Score with real benefits for good guests
  • Split & Invite: group bookings settled over UPI
  • Monetise the trip, not the room night
Headline results
North Star: verified trips per active traveller per year Take rate 15.5% → 13%, contribution/booking +~10% 4 MVPs in one clickable prototype RICE-ranked roadmap with kill criteria

Analytics

Zomato — Funnel, Cohort & North Star Analysis

Where Tier-1 users give up between opening the app and getting their food
Funnel finding

Of 1,000 app opens, 837 users are lost, and 700 leave in the first three steps, before they ever see a price. The real problem isn't checkout; it's a hungry user who can't decide what to eat.

Cohort finding

Across 6 cohorts, discount-only users almost all leave within a year (11 of 100 remain vs 58 for full-price users), and new users drop fastest, so the first three orders matter most.

Metrics & plan
North Star: monthly repeat delivered orders Metric ladder for 13 hypotheses 12-month plan: 170 → ~206 orders per 1,000 opens (+21%)

Figures are illustrative examples built to show the method, not real Zomato data.

PRD

StreakSquad — VitaFit Retention System

A gamification + community-challenges PRD to fix Day-14 churn
Problem

VitaFit loses users in their first 14 days — the exact window habits form. Root causes: no visible reward for showing up, and solo, anonymous sessions with zero social accountability.

Approach

Designed two systems around one shared trigger — completing a session feeds a personal rewards engine (points, streaks, tiers) and a social challenge engine (squads, leaderboards) at the same time.

Rollout plan

Five phases: discovery & analytics validation → clickable Figma prototype & usability testing → MVP build → randomized A/B test on Day-14 retention → gated full rollout.

Target outcomes
Day-14 retention: +10–15 pts Sessions/user/week: 2 → 4 Guardrail: no rise in burnout complaints
User research

Uber — Find My Ride

Diagnosing why pickups fail in malls, campuses, and stations
Research goal

Understand root causes of rider-driver pickup friction, where it happens most, and its impact on wait times and cancellations — before proposing any fix.

Method

Structured rider interviews walking through the full booking-to-pickup journey, probing confidence, confusion points, and how issues actually got resolved.

Key finding

The core problem isn't booking — it's pickup coordination in complex venues, where a map pin alone isn't enough and riders/drivers default to phone calls to find each other.

Recommendations shipped in the write-up
  • Smart pickup-point recommendation using venue access data, not just GPS
  • Landmark-based pickup guidance (gates, entrances, stores)
  • Priority driver-matching for historically high-friction locations
UX audit

Nykaa — Purchase Journey Evaluation

An end-to-end usability walkthrough of buying a dry shampoo
Method

Walked the full journey — search, filter, browse, product detail, checkout — logging what felt smooth, confusing, slow, or frustrating at each step, then validated against real user conversations.

Key finding

Discovery wasn't the problem — comparison was. Users spent more time and hesitation distinguishing near-identical products than finding them in the first place.

Recommendations
  • Side-by-side comparison feature for similar products
  • A prominent "Best For [hair type]" badge on listings
  • Auto-apply the best available coupon instead of showing dozens
Product strategy

Corporate Learning Platform

A 3-year land-and-expand strategy for a B2B SaaS onboarding product
Core problem

VC-backed startups (50–500 employees) hire fast but onboard inconsistently — training is scattered across docs, videos, and manager memory, slowing time-to-productivity.

Strategic bet

Don't compete with enterprise LMS on content libraries. Land narrowly on onboarding, then expand into team training, continuous learning, and workforce capability management.

Defensibility built into the plan
