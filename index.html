PRD

StreakSquad — VitaFit Retention System

A gamification + community-challenges PRD to fix Day-14 churn
Problem

VitaFit loses users in their first 14 days — the exact window habits form. Root causes: no visible reward for showing up, and solo, anonymous sessions with zero social accountability.

Approach

Designed two systems around one shared trigger — completing a session feeds a personal rewards engine (points, streaks, tiers) and a social challenge engine (squads, leaderboards) at the same time.

Rollout plan

Five phases: discovery & analytics validation → clickable Figma prototype & usability testing → MVP build → randomized A/B test on Day-14 retention → gated full rollout.

Target outcomes
Day-14 retention: +10–15 pts Sessions/user/week: 2 → 4 Guardrail: no rise in burnout complaints
User research

Uber — Find My Ride

Diagnosing why pickups fail in malls, campuses, and stations
Research goal

Understand root causes of rider-driver pickup friction, where it happens most, and its impact on wait times and cancellations — before proposing any fix.

Method

Structured rider interviews walking through the full booking-to-pickup journey, probing confidence, confusion points, and how issues actually got resolved.

Key finding

The core problem isn't booking — it's pickup coordination in complex venues, where a map pin alone isn't enough and riders/drivers default to phone calls to find each other.

Recommendations shipped in the write-up
  • Smart pickup-point recommendation using venue access data, not just GPS
  • Landmark-based pickup guidance (gates, entrances, stores)
  • Priority driver-matching for historically high-friction locations
UX audit

Nykaa — Purchase Journey Evaluation

An end-to-end usability walkthrough of buying a dry shampoo
Method

Walked the full journey — search, filter, browse, product detail, checkout — logging what felt smooth, confusing, slow, or frustrating at each step, then validated against real user conversations.

Key finding

Discovery wasn't the problem — comparison was. Users spent more time and hesitation distinguishing near-identical products than finding them in the first place.

Recommendations
  • Side-by-side comparison feature for similar products
  • A prominent "Best For [hair type]" badge on listings
  • Auto-apply the best available coupon instead of showing dozens
Product strategy

Corporate Learning Platform

A 3-year land-and-expand strategy for a B2B SaaS onboarding product
Core problem

VC-backed startups (50–500 employees) hire fast but onboard inconsistently — training is scattered across docs, videos, and manager memory, slowing time-to-productivity.

Strategic bet

Don't compete with enterprise LMS on content libraries. Land narrowly on onboarding, then expand into team training, continuous learning, and workforce capability management.

Defensibility built into the plan
